About Me

Dr Anne Ndirangu PhD


Dr Anne has over 30 years’ experience gained from Education and Financial sector both in the private and public sector. She has worked in various institutions which include: Thunguma Academy(Principal), Wamuritu Secondary School(Commerce teacher), Ruthagati High School, St Edward High School(Nairobi),Faulu Kenya Microfinance Bank and ECLOF Kenya Microfinance.


Dr Anne is a (CHRP-K)Certified Human Resource Professional and a full member of the Institute of Human Resource Management(IHRM).She consults widely in financial literacy with local and international Non-Governmental Organization. She represented the microfinance sector in Kenya in Credit & Risk curriculum development with Financial Sector Deepening (Africa). Dr Anne has widely researched on youth enterprises has published 4 articles in peer refereed journals.


She is a business advisor and mentor for the youths and women across different sectors. She is also the chairperson in the technical team for Banking & other financial services at Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) for ISO certifications. She gives an oversight role in church as an elder in-charge of Professionals & Business community where capacity building, mentorship and networking are well propagated for a holistic personal development. She further examines and supervises PhD and Master’s Thesis for Strathmore and PAC Universities.

Projects

Youth Enterprise Growth & Innovation Program

Dr. Anne Ndirangu leads the Youth Enterprise Growth & Innovation Program, a project designed to equip young entrepreneurs with practical business skills, mentorship, and access to startup and growth capital.

Youth Enterprise Growth & Innovation Program

The program focuses on nurturing innovative ideas, strengthening business models, and building financial discipline among youth-owned enterprises.
Through training, coaching, and linkages to markets and financial institutions, the initiative supports sustainable youth businesses that create jobs and drive economic inclusion.

KJET

Dr. Anne Ndirangu is actively involved in the Kenya Jobs & Economic Transformation (KJET) Project, an initiative focused on strengthening Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) as a key driver of inclusive economic growth in Kenya.

KJET

Through this project, she provides strategic business advisory, capacity building, and mentorship to entrepreneurs, enabling them to build resilient enterprises, improve operational efficiency, and access sustainable financing.
By supporting MSMEs to formalize, scale, and connect to viable markets, Dr. Anne contributes to job creation, increased productivity, and long-term economic transformation, particularly for youth- and women-led businesses across the country.

Business Coach

With over 30 years of experience across education and the financial sector, Dr. Anne Ndirangu offers business training that blends academic excellence, practical expertise, and hands-on mentorship. Her training equips entrepreneurs, professionals, and organizations with the skills to develop business strategies, enhance financial literacy, and drive sustainable growth.

Business Coach

Her programs are ideal for those seeking guidance in business development, strategic planning, and personal growth in professional settings.

PDI (Personal Development Initiatives)

Founded by Dr. Anne Ndirangu, PDI is a transformative mentorship platform dedicated to helping individuals discover their true potential and achieve personal and professional growth. At PDI, we guide participants to understand who they truly are, uncover hidden strengths, and gain the confidence to step into areas they have never explored before.

PDI (Personal Development Initiatives)

Our approach combines mentorship, coaching, and practical exercises that empower individuals to overcome self-doubt, build skills, and take actionable steps toward their goals. We take joy in walking alongside each person as they tackle challenges they once felt inadequate about, helping them develop competence and confidence. Within a short period, participants experience tangible growth, improved self-belief, and the motivation to pursue new opportunities with clarity and purpose.
Through weekly virtual sessions, workshops, and one-on-one guidance, PDI serves entrepreneurs, professionals, and youth, fostering holistic personal development and enabling lasting impact in both personal and professional life.
